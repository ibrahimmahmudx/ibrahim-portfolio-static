About

I am a 16-year-old student from Bangladesh, in Class 10 in the Science group, preparing for the SSC exam in 2027.

My main interests are cybersecurity — information security, web and network security, ethical hacking, and vulnerability assessment — and artificial intelligence: how large models reason, AI agents, and APIs. I build my websites and projects with AI tools, and I learn by taking real systems apart and putting them back together.

My long-term goal is to study Computer Science and Engineering and become a highly skilled professional in AI and cybersecurity. My guiding line is simple: “Build a world where hacking is no subject.”
